WELCOME TO GAZE STUDIO

A different perspective
on making things.

Draw by selecting points with your eyes or with a mouse.

  1. Choose a tool.

    Use Four points for numbered targets or Line tool for any two positions on the canvas.

  2. Calibrate eye tracking.

    Look at each of nine dots and click it five times. Recalibrate after switching tools or changing your position or window size.

  3. Draw.

    Hold until the target or cursor ring fills. In Line tool, a dashed line previews the stroke after you set the start. Hold on the end point to finish.

No camera? Try mouse mode. Hover over the canvas to select a point. In Four points, you can also tap a target or press 1–4. Space pauses, Esc leaves focus mode.

Camera frames are processed locally by WebGazer. Tracking code and models load from third-party hosts. Only your drawing is saved in this browser.
